Design pass 05 Import pipeline · Re-entry engine · Journeys · Handoff

A spreadsheet becomes a methodology

The last architectural pass. Imported programs default to strict but the model carries the adaptive flag from day one. Re-entry is a configurable engine with its own reason codes, not the 90% example. Import runs through a validated pipeline that never writes interpreted content straight into the canonical model, and every prescription keeps a path back to its source cell. The full specification is in the handoff document.

Availability now describes opportunities — days, time per day, doubles, the occasional long one — and the planner works out how many Sessions fit inside them. Program selection leads with a recommendation and its reason, so nobody has to know what a mesocycle is to start. Priority is derived from the phase, not asked for. 05

Open questions before I go wider

Does a session ever run two modalities at once?
Squat + metcon is one session with two blocks. Strength in the morning and a run at night is two sessions on one day. Today needs to show both without becoming a dashboard.
Who owns the RIR value — you or the engine?
Logging RIR per set is three taps you may not want mid-set. Alternative: log it once per exercise, and only when the set falls outside the target range.
How much can the plan change itself?
A missed session can silently reshuffle, or always ask. I've assumed it asks once and remembers your preference — needs your call before I design the recovery flow.
Is the deload a phase or a week?
Modelled here as a short phase on the spine. Program-owned deload weeks are the alternative, and the two behave differently when a phase gets cut short.
Should scaling be sticky?
Knee raises for toes-to-bar: remembered for every future workout, or chosen each time? Sticky is fewer taps but hides the moment you're ready for Rx.
Not yet designed
Onboarding and goal creation, calendar, EMOM and For Time execution, completion and PR moment, exercise and benchmark detail, program library, missed-workout recovery, pause and return, HealthKit permissions, settings. Light mode.

Next: pick a navigation structure, answer what you can above, and I'll build the remaining states plus the five journey prototypes — then the handoff bundle.
